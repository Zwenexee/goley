Revival - music
===============

The lobby plays Revival's radio station: one playlist the team curates, and
everyone hears the same track at the same moment. The tracks are downloaded
into the station\ folder next to this file and kept up to date on their own;
you do not need to touch it.

  - Press Tab in the lobby for the player; the chevron opens the card with
    the next tracks and the volume slider. Ctrl+Tab opens the UI inspector.
  - A track that is still downloading is silent until it has arrived; that
    happens once per new track, then it plays from disk.
  - No internet, or the station is empty: your own files play instead.

Your own music: put .mp3 or .wav files in THIS folder and they play, shuffled
at every launch, whenever the station is unavailable. The file name is the
title; underscores become spaces. The folder is read once at start.

This folder belongs to you. The launcher creates it once and never touches
it again, so nothing you put here is replaced or removed by an update.
